Theoremsylbbr 208 A mixed syllogism inference from two biconditionals.

Note on the various syllogism-like statements in set.mm. The hypothetical syllogism syl 18 infers an implication from two implications (and there are 3syl 19 and 4syl 20 for chaining more inferences). There are four inferences inferring an implication from one implication and one biconditional: sylbi 190, sylib 191, sylbir 207, sylibr 206; four inferences inferring an implication from two biconditionals: sylbb 192, sylbbr 208, sylbb1 209, sylbb2 210; four inferences inferring a biconditional from two biconditionals: bitri 232, bitr2i 233, bitr3i 234, bitr4i 235 (and more for chaining more biconditionals). There are also closed forms and deduction versions of these, like, among many others, syl5 44, syl6 45, bitrid 237, bitrdi 236 and variants. (Contributed by BJ, 21-Apr-2019.)
