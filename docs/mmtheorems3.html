Theoremsylbbr 204 A mixed syllogism inference from two biconditionals.

Note on the various syllogism-like statements in set.mm. The hypothetical syllogism syl 18 infers an implication from two implications (and there are 3syl 19 and 4syl 20 for chaining more inferences). There are four inferences inferring an implication from one implication and one biconditional: sylbi 186, sylib 187, sylbir 203, sylibr 202; four inferences inferring an implication from two biconditionals: sylbb 188, sylbbr 204, sylbb1 205, sylbb2 206; four inferences inferring a biconditional from two biconditionals: bitri 228, bitr2i 229, bitr3i 230, bitr4i 231 (and more for chaining more biconditionals). There are also closed forms and deduction versions of these, like, among many others, syl5 44, syl6 45, bitrid 233, bitrdi 232 and variants. (Contributed by BJ, 21-Apr-2019.)
